# Who is IMPACT?

Created in 2010, IMPACT is a Geneva-based NGO and the largest independent data provider in contexts of crisis. IMPACT aims to support a range of stakeholders in making better, more informed decisions in humanitarian, stabilisation, and development contexts.

IMPACT has a footprint in more than 30 countries, with more than 400 staff, including those working at its HQ in Geneva. More information can be found on [impact-initiatives.org](https://www.impact-initiatives.org/).

## What does IMPACT do?

IMPACT's work is conducted through three initiatives:

- **REACH** strengthens evidence-based humanitarian decision-making through efficient data collection, management, and analysis – before, during, and after an emergency.
- **PANDA** improves the impact of humanitarian and development interventions through programme design, assessments, and monitoring & evaluation.
- **AGORA** promotes localised and multi-sectoral aid action in support of the recovery and stabilisation of crisis-affected communities, in partnership with local stakeholders.

## Information products

REACH creates information products that can be used to inform the humanitarian response during a crisis. On average, IMPACT publishes more than 2,000 information products a year. These include both visual and written products such as datasets, maps, dashboards, reports, and factsheets.

The vast majority of IMPACT's information products rely on primary data collection in the field, qualitative and/or quantitative, on the needs and displacement patterns of populations affected by crisis. Data collection methods commonly used by IMPACT to collect qualitative data include Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) with affected communities and semi-structured Key Informant Interviews (KIIs).
